Privacy Policy for Transcriber

Last updated: October 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Transcriber ("the Application", "we", "us", or "our"), developed by independent developer Mirko Dimartino, collects, uses, and safeguards information when you use our Android application.

We are committed to respecting your privacy and adhering to the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and Google Play Store Developer Policies.

1. Information We Process and How We Use It

a. Audio Files and Transcriptions (Core Functionality)

To provide its primary service, Transcriber processes audio files shared from third-party messaging apps to generate text transcripts.

  • Wit.ai Integration & Anonymization: The speech-to-text processing is not performed locally. The audio files you choose to transcribe are securely transmitted to Wit.ai services (provided by Meta Platforms, Inc.) for transcription. Before transmission, audio files undergo a voice anonymization process.
  • No Account Tracking: The Application does not require you to create a user account. Therefore, any audio file processed by Wit.ai is completely anonymous and cannot be linked back to you (no "user-audio" tracking).
  • No Developer Storage: While your audio is transmitted to Wit.ai for processing, we (the Developer) never collect, store, or transmit your personal audio files or the resulting text transcripts to our personal servers. Once the transcription is completed and displayed on your device, the Application does not retain the original media.

b. In-App Purchases and Subscription Data

In-app billing and subscription lifecycles are processed directly via the Google Play In-App Billing API.

  • We do not store or have access to your credit card numbers, bank details, or direct payment data.
  • The Application queries Google Play's inventory locally to verify whether you hold an active Premium license.

3. Data Retention and Security

  • Because we do not store your audio files or personal messages on our own remote servers, there is no risk of your private conversations being compromised from our database.
  • We implement industry-standard local security measures to protect against unauthorized access on your device.

4. Your Rights under GDPR

If you reside within the European Economic Area (EEA), you possess full rights under the GDPR:

  • Right to access, rectify, or erase any personal data held locally.
  • Right to lodge a complaint with a competent supervisory data protection authority.
